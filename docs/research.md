Patterns that work (6 lines):
1. 5-7 top labels in plain words (Home / About / Programs / Resources / News / Contact); no jargon.
2. Phone number, address and open hours visible on every page header or footer.
3. Hall rental gets its own top-level page: capacity, rules, photos, one clear action (we add the inquiry form).
4. Footer repeats the menu + contact block, so no one has to scroll back up.
5. Calendar plus a printable/PDF version; older members still print.
6. Members/admin login kept out of the main nav (footer or last item); "Find a Post"/membership eligibility in one plain sentence.

## 3. VFW emblem / generic logo
- National seal = eight-pointed Cross of Malta; sun rays between the arms; Great Seal of the U.S. (American eagle) at the centre; ring text "Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States". Eight points = the Beatitudes; lineage to Knights of St. John/Crusades. [V] https://www.vfw.org/media-and-events/latest-releases/archives/2021/2/the-significance-of-the-cross-of-malta
- Colours: official seal has no published colour spec. Symbolism: red = danger/bloodshed and energy of service; gold = achievements, valor, eligibility. [V] https://VFW.org/vfwmarks . 2018 new brand logo (no cross) uses gold + red. [S] https://vfw.org/media-and-events/latest-releases/archives/2018/11/vfw-announces-new-brand-identity
- Trademark: VFW has held the Cross of Malta since 1931; the name/emblem of a federally chartered veterans organization are protected by federal law; the vfwmarks page limits the seal to "special and distinguished situations" and says nothing about posts. [V/S] Treat it as protected: do NOT draw the Cross of Malta, rays-around-seal, the "VFW" wordmark lockup, or "Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States" ring text.
- Safe generic ideas (our own geometry): a shield or rounded badge (not an 8-point cross); a plain 4-arm or star-in-circle shape is OK but avoid Maltese arms; a 5-point star row or single star; a simple eagle head/wings silhouette or flag-stripes band; ring/banner text "POST 6493" and "WARMINSTER, PA"; motto "No One Does More for Veterans" (their own Facebook motto, check it is not a VFW slogan before putting it in the logo, keep it in site copy instead). Use muted navy / charcoal with antique gold and muted red. Say "VFW Post 6493" in text only, as a plain identity, not in a lockup that mimics the national mark.
- Not legal advice; an owner may confirm with the Post/Department of PA.

National history (cited):
- Sept 29, 1899: veterans of the Spanish-American War (14 of the 17th Infantry per VFW release; other VFW text says 13) met in Columbus, OH and formed the American Veterans of Foreign Service; separate groups also formed in Denver CO and Altoona PA. [V] https://www.vfw.org/media-and-events/latest-releases/archives/2024/9/vfw-was-born-of-battle
- Name "Veterans of Foreign Wars of the U.S." since Aug 1, 1914 [S]; congressionally chartered May 28, 1936 (74th Congress) [S] (search summary of VFW pages: https://www.vfw.org/vfw126); purposes fraternal, patriotic, historical, charitable, educational.
- 1917 helped lead War Risk Insurance Act amendments; 1919 National Service Bureau created (now National Veterans Service); 1944 GI Bill of Rights; recent: PACT Act. [V] same "born of battle" page.
- 1922 first national Buddy Poppy distribution; 1924 "Buddy Poppy" registered; >1 billion distributed. [V] https://www.vfw.org/community/community-initiatives/buddy-poppy
- 2007+ partnerships with Burger King and Sport Clips; Unmet Needs created 2004. [S]
- 125th anniversary Sept 2024. [V]
